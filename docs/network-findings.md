# Network findings — Generation Jihad AfPak corpus (93 episodes, Mar 2020 – Oct 2026)

All statements below are **as discussed on the podcast** (FDD Long War Journal), machine-transcribed and
extracted; they are claims with provenance, not adjudicated facts. Numbers are from `db/afpak.sqlite`
as built by `scripts/build_db.py`. Tables follow the narrative.

## What the corpus reveals

**Hubs.** Two organizations anchor the whole graph — al-Qaeda (92/93 episodes, ~1,570 edges) and the
Taliban (92/93, ~1,550). The Haqqani Network (62 episodes), Islamic State / ISKP (78 / 46), TTP (42) and
AQAP (40) form the second tier. Among persons, Sirajuddin Haqqani (46 episodes, 212 edges) and
Ayman al-Zawahiri (68 episodes, 194 edges) are the most connected, followed by Osama bin Laden,
Saif al-Adel, Jalaluddin Haqqani, Hibatullah Akhundzada and Mullah Omar.

**The Taliban–al-Qaeda–Haqqani triangle.** The single most repeated analytic thread: the Haqqani
Network is described as the hinge between the Taliban's political leadership and al-Qaeda's core, with
Sirajuddin Haqqani simultaneously Taliban deputy emir / interior minister and an al-Qaeda ally. Shelter
edges run Taliban → Pakistan (ISI / "Pakistani state"), al-Qaeda → Taliban/Haqqani, AQIS and TTP →
Taliban, TIP/Uyghurs → Taliban. Zawahiri's presence in Kabul (killed July 2022 in a Haqqani-linked
safe house) is the anchor event for this triangle.

**Intermarriage as network glue.** Family edges tie the al-Qaeda "houses" together: Hamza bin Laden
married Miriam, daughter of Abu Muhammad al-Masri (in Iran); Abd al-Rahman al-Maghrebi is Zawahiri's
son-in-law; Abu al-Khayr al-Masri is tied to Zawahiri by marriage; Haqqani–al-Qaeda intermarriage is
asserted going back to the 1980s. The Haqqani family itself (Jalaluddin → Sirajuddin, Anas, Khalil,
Ibrahim) and the Massoud family (Ahmad Shah → Ahmad) are the densest kinship clusters.

**Iran as sanctuary.** Saif al-Adel, Abu Muhammad al-Masri, Abu al-Khayr al-Masri, Hamza bin Laden and
Zawahiri's deputies are repeatedly placed in Iran under IRGC/Quds Force custody or protection; this is
the main Iran–AfPak bridge in the data and is marked `stated` in most episodes.

**Succession chains.** Taliban: Omar → Mansour → Akhundzada (with Yaqoob and Sirajuddin as deputies).
al-Qaeda: bin Laden → Zawahiri → (Saif al-Adel, de facto). AQAP: Wuhayshi → Rimi → Batarfi. ISKP:
successive governors to Sanaullah Ghafari. TTP: Baitullah → Hakimullah → Fazlullah → Noor Wali Mehsud.
These are captured as `succeeded`/`leads`/`deputy_of` edges with dates where stated.

**Geography.** Kabul, Kandahar, Helmand, Kunar/Nuristan, Waziristan, Quetta/Peshawar, Doha, Tora Bora,
Abbottabad and the Iranian border zone are the recurring hubs; Kunar/Nuristan is the recurrent
al-Qaeda/ISKP/TTP sanctuary belt.

**Status changes.** 1,394 dated events and ~1,150 per-episode status rows let the GUI render "status as
of" — e.g. Zawahiri alive (2020–mid-2022) → killed; Hamza bin Laden reported killed (2019) but
repeatedly discussed as a possible survivor; Saif al-Adel's location contested (Iran vs Afghanistan).

**Official profiles layered on the podcast network.** Two public-record sources are attached to the same
entity IDs: the UN Security Council 1988 (Taliban) Sanctions List — all 140 listings, of which 20 are tied to the
Haqqani Network entity listing (TAe.012) — and the 54 items on the FBI's Terrorism wanted page. The UN COMMENTS
fields state the Haqqani family structure directly (Jalaluddin TAi.040 father of Sirajuddin TAi.144 and Nasiruddin
TAi.146; brother of Khalil TAi.150 and Mohammad Ibrahim Omari TAi.042; Khalil uncle of Sirajuddin) plus the command
layer under Sirajuddin (Abdul Aziz Abbasin, Abdul Rauf Zakir, Ahmed Jan Wazir, Fazl Rabi, Bakht Gul, Yahya Haqqani,
Saidullah Jan, Muhammad Omar Zadran); these become `relationships.source='un_1988'` edges with the UN sentence as the
quote. Sixteen UN listings and six FBI subjects were matched by hand to podcast figures (Sirajuddin Haqqani carries all
three sources; Saif al-Adel, Hamza al-Ghamdi, Sajid Mir, Abd al-Rahman al-Maghrebi and Jehad Serwan Mostafa carry FBI
biodata; Omar, Mansour, Baradar, Muttaqi, Hassan Akhund, Stanikzai, Wasiq, Khairkhwa, Fazl carry UN biodata). Everything
else on those lists is loaded as its own entity so the GUI can show the full official list without over-merging.

**What the data cannot do.** No coordinates, identifiers or imagery; `allied_with`/`rival_of` are
over-generated by the extractor for co-mentioned organizations (treat as weak unless quoted); about half of the
episodes (47 of 93) were hand-extracted after the Anthropic account ran out of credits (sparser on organizations
and places — see `raw/reports/`).
