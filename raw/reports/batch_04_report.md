## Analyst summary — networks revealed in these episodes

**Key individuals (role / affiliation / family)**
1. **Saif al-Adel** — AQ emir, Egyptian; based in Iran as a guest of the Iranian government (286, 336 — the Aug-2026 UN report says so explicitly; only Iran dissents). Rose #3→#2 after Abu Muhammad al-Masri's killing in Iran, leader after Zawahiri. Directs Hurras al-Din (92); his son died in Yemen working with AQAP (~2024, 336). Instrument of AQ's partial integration into Iran's "axis of resistance".
2. **Ayman al-Zawahiri** — AQ emir until killed by the US in Kabul, 31 Jul 2022; sheltered by the Haqqanis; Taliban still deny he was there (189, 286).
3. **Sirajuddin Haqqani** — Taliban interior minister, deputy emir, head of the Haqqani Network; son of Jalaluddin Haqqani; brother Anas Haqqani; uncle Khalil Haqqani (231/242). Reunited the Taliban after the 2015 Mullah Omar disclosure; "moderate" NYT framing debunked (231, 242); corralled by Hibatullah in 2025 after dissent on girls' education; hosts think he backs Yaqoob rather than aspiring to be emir (286).
4. **Hibatullah Akhundzada** — Taliban supreme leader; rules inaccessibly from Kandahar via gatekeepers; ICC-indicted with chief justice **Abdul Hakim Haqqani** for gender persecution (286).
5. **Mullah Yaqoob** — defence minister, deputy emir, son of Mullah Omar; hosts' favourite to succeed Hibatullah (286).
6. **Mullah Omar** — Taliban founder; died 2013, death hidden two years; disclosure in 2015 drove defections to ISKP (231, 286).
7. **Sher Mohammad Abbas Stanikzai** — ex-deputy FM, effectively exiled for opposing the girls' education ban (286).
8. **Nasser al-Wuhayshi → Qasim al-Raymi → Khalid Batarfi → Saad al-Awlaki** — AQAP succession (4, 336); Raymi killed Jan 2020, Batarfi named Feb 2020; Batarfi died ~2024. **Ibrahim al-Qosi** — AQAP shura member and AQ's leading ideologue, wrongly called "head of AQAP" in the UN report (336).
9. **Abu Muhammad al-Masri / Abd al-Rahman al-Maghrebi** — Hittin Committee in Iran; al-Masri assassinated by Israel in Iran; al-Maghrebi now AQ #3, still in Iran (336).
10. **Abdul Qadir Mumin** — IS Somalia head, confirmed by the UN as IS caliph (first African); Roggio skeptical (242, 336). **Abu Maryam al-Nuki** — IS deputy/GDP head, Nigerian, killed by the US in Nigeria May 2026 (336). **Abu Khadija** — earlier IS leadership candidate (242).
11. **Sanaullah Ghafari** — ISKP emir; harmonious ties with the IS al-Sadiq office (286).
12. **Noor Wali Mehsud** — TTP emir; TTP reabsorbed ~30 factions since 2020 and shelters in Afghanistan (92, 189). **Hakimullah Mehsud** (ex-TTP emir, operated from Afghanistan; wrongly reported dead 2009-10) and **Mullah Fazlullah** (92).
13. **Hafiz Gul Bahadur** — North Waziristan Taliban leader, now allied with Lashkar-e-Islam as Ittehad-ul-Mujahideen Pakistan (286).
14. **Abdul Haq al-Turkistani** — TIP emir, SDGT, on AQ's central shura, based in Afghanistan; TIP fighters folded into the Syrian army (189, 336).
15. **Ahmad Massoud** — NRF leader, son of Ahmad Shah Massoud (175); **Yasin Zia** (AFF). **Ahmed al-Sharaa** — ex-AQ Syria chief, now Syrian president (286, 336).
16. **Asim Umar** (AQIS founding emir, killed 2019, with a Haqqani escort) and **Osama Mahmood** (successor) (28, 189); **Abu Ikhlas al-Masri**, **Qari Zia Rahman**, **Ilyas Kashmiri** as AQ–Taliban integration examples (28).

**Organizational relationships.** Taliban ↔ al-Qaeda: unchanged UN assessment — camps in 12–13 provinces (four in Ghazni; new camp at Barg-i-Matal, Nuristan), madrasas, media centre, depot, senior commanders in Kabul (286, 336). Taliban/AQIS/TTP form "an aggressive terrorist alliance" projecting violence into Pakistan; AQIS (200–300, hosts say too low) has blended with and trains TTP; one member state says AQ also trained the BLA (286, 336). Haqqani Network = AQ's historic protector and the Taliban's foreign-relations channel. Taliban restrain TIP/Ansarullah/IMU from overt attacks to court China and Central Asia; TIP massing in Badakhshan. ISKP (~2,000) suppressed but not eliminated; increasingly Tajik/Central Asian; external attacks (Kerman, Crocus) run through the al-Sadiq office, which recent UN reports omit. AQ ↔ Iran: Hittin Committee hosted for years; Houthi–AQAP truce and Houthi–Shabaab ties flow from it. AQ global: AQIM's $50M UAE ransom shared to JNIM and AQ central — evidence against the UN's "marginalized leadership" line. Pakistan ↔ Taliban: patron turned victim ("scorpion and frog"); Durand Line clashes nearly became war in 2025, Turkey-brokered truce. Taliban/Syria integrating former jihadists into security forces (286).

**Key places.** Kabul (AQ commanders, Zawahiri strike); Kandahar (Hibatullah's seat); Ghazni, Nuristan/Barg-i-Matal, Badakhshan, Kunar, Shorabak (AQ/TIP camps); North Waziristan; Durand Line; Panjshir/Andarab (NRF); Iran/Tehran (AQ leadership); Kerman and Crocus City Hall (ISKP external ops); Yemen (AQAP/Houthi truce); Puntland/Somalia (IS caliph, Shabaab 35–40% control); Mali/Burkina Faso/Niger (JNIM); Lake Chad Basin (ISWAP); al-Hol (Syria).
