1. (C) SUMMARY: TALIBAN LEADER MULLAH OMAR HAS BEEN CITED AS
MAKING RECENT REMARKS -- SOME CONFIRMED, SOME NOT -- THAT HAVE LED
TO QUESTIONS WHETHER HIS THINKING IS EVOLVING IN A PAN-ISLAMIST
DIRECTION. THESE REMARKS TEND TO CUT AGAINST THE GRAIN OF WHAT WE
HAD THOUGHT WE KNEW ABOUT OMAR, WHOM MOST OBSERVERS HAVE PICTURED
AS A "TRADITIONALIST," INWARD-LOOKING SUNNI MUSLIM. GIVEN THE
CONTENT OF THESE REMARKS, IT IS POSSIBLE THAT OMAR MAY HAVE COME
UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF TERRORIST USAMA BIN LADIN (OR PERHAPS OTHER
RADICALS), ALTHOUGH THAT IS SPECULATION. THE POSSIBILITY OF OMAR
BEING INFLUENCED IN SUCH A WAY WOULD NOT BE UNIQUE -- THERE ARE
MANY EXAMPLES OF AFGHAN MULLAHS BEING RADICALIZED WHEN EXPOSED TO
PAN-ISLAMIC THOUGHT. THE REGIONAL RAMIFICATIONS OF A RADICAL DRIFT
BY OMAR COULD MEAN THAT WHEN AND IF THE TALIBAN SETTLE THE CURRENT
WAR THAT THEY GO ON TO SEEK NEW CHALLENGES IN KASHMIR, CENTRAL
ASIA, OR EVEN PAKISTAN. END SUMMARY.

5. (S) VIEWED HISTORICALLY, THE PURPORTED OMAR/BIN LADIN
RELATIONSHIP WOULD NOT BE THE FIRST TIME IN PASHTUN HISTORY THAT A
MULLAH CAME FACE-TO-FACE WITH RADICAL TRENDS OF ANTI-WESTERN
ISLAMIC THINKING AND BECAME INFLUENCED BY THEM. THE HISTORY OF THE
REGION (AFGHANISTAN AND THE NORTH-WEST FRONTIER PROVINCE) IS
REPLETE WITH SUCH INSTANCES. FOR EXAMPLE, EARLIER THIS CENTURY,
AFGHAN MULLAHS HEARD THAT THE "CALIPHATE" (THE OTTOMAN EMPIRE) IN
ISTANBUL WAS UNDER THREAT FROM THE WEST, SO THEY CAME OUT OF THE
WOODWORK TO PRESSURE THE AFGHAN GOVERNMENT TO TAKE A STAND AGAINST
THE BRITISH. THERE HAVE ALSO BEEN MANY INSTANCES IN THE NORTH-WEST
FRONTIER OF MULLAHS DRIFTING TOWARD PAN-ISLAMIC THOUGHT, AND TAKING
TO THE WARPATH AGAINST THE BRITISH OR EVEN PAKISTANI GOVERNMENT , .
WHICH THEY DEFINED AS "KAFIR" OR "HERETICAL," RESPECTIVELY. IN THE
1930'S AND 1940'S IT TOOK THE BRITISH AND AFGHAN AUTHORITIES YEARS
