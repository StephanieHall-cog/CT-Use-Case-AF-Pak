1. This is an action request. Please see paragraph 3.
2. Summary: The United States prenotified (reftels) in June
to UNSC Members and other key UN Member States its intention ~
to add the names of a Yemen-based U.S. person and al-Qa'ida
in the Arab Peninsula (AQAP) leader Anwar al-Aulaqi and three
Pakistan-based terrorist leaders Nasiruddin Haqqani, Gul Agha
Ishakzai, and Amir Abdullah to the Committee's list of
individuals and entities associated with Usama bin Laden, the
Taliban or al-Qaida. Listed individuals/entities are subject
to UN Security Council mandated sanctions, including a travel
ban, arms embargo, and assets freeze. AQAP was listed by the
UN 1267 Committee earlier this year under reference number
QE.A.129.10. End Summary.
3. Action request: USUN is requested on or about July 1 to
nominate for listing by the UN 1267 Committee Anwar
al-Aulaqi. USUN is further requested on or about July 6
nominate for listing Nasir Haqqani, Gul Agha Ishakzai, and
Amir Abdullah. USUN should provide the following identifier ‘
and background’ information to support these nominations:
Identifiers information

(1) Anwar al-Aulagi
AKA: Anwar al-Awlaki
AKA: Anwar al-Awlaqi
AKA: Anwar Nasser Aulaqi
AKA: Anwar Nasser Abdullah Aulaqgi
Citizenship: United States
Citizenship: Yemen
DOB: April 21, 1971
Alternate DOB: April 22, 1971
POB: Las Cruces, New Mexico :
Anwar al-Aulagi, a dual U.S.-Yemeni citizen, is a leader of
al-Qa'ida in the Arabian Peninsula (AQAP),.a Yemen-based
terrorist group that has claimed responsibility for numerous
terrorist acts against Saudi, Korean, Yemeni, and U.S.
targets since its inception in January 2009. Aulaqi has
pledged an oath of loyalty to AQAP emir, Nasir al-Wahishi
(listed by the UN 1267 Committee earlier this year under
reference number QI.A.274.10), and is playing a key role in

UNCLASSIFIED

[PDF p.3]
setting the strategic direction for AQAP. Aulaqi has also
recruited individuals to join AQAP, facilitated training at UNCLASSIFIED
camps in Yemen in support of acts of terrorism, and helped
focus AQAP's attention on planning attacks on U.S. interests.
Since late 2009, Aulagi has taken on an increasingly
operational role in the group, including preparing Umar
Farouk Abdulmutallab, who attempted to detonate an incendiary
device aboard a Northwest airlines flight from Amsterdam to
Detroit on Christmas Day 2009, for his operation. In November
2009, while in Yemen, Abdulmutallab swore allegiance to the
emir of AQAP and shortly thereafter received instructions
from Aulaqi to detonate an explosive device aboard a U.S.
airplane over U.S. airspace. After providing this direction,
Aulagi told Abdulmutallab to travel to Ma'rib Governorate,
Yemen, where Abdulmutallab obtained the explosive device he
used in the attempted Christmas Day attack.
Aulagi was imprisoned in Yemen in 2006 on charges of
kidnapping for ransom and being involved in an al-Qa'ida plot
to kidnap a U.S. official, but was released from jail in
December 2007 and subsequently went into hiding in Yemen.
(2) Name: Gul Agha Ishakzai
AKA: Mullah Gul Agha
AKA: Mullah Gul Agha Akhund
AKA: Hidayatullah
AKA: Haji Hidayatullah
AKA: Hayadatullah
DOB: circa 1972
POB: Band Timor, Qandahar, Afghanistan
Gul Agha is the head of the Taliban's financial
commission and is part of a recently-created Taliban council
that coordinates the collection of zakat (Islamic tax) from
Baluchistan Province, Pakistan. Gul Agha has been involved in
the payment of medical care for Taliban fighters and their
families. Gul Agha has also collected money for suicide
attacks in Qandahar, Afghanistan.’
A childhood friend of Taliban leader Mullah Mohammad .
Omar (listed by the UN 1267 Committee on January 31, 2001),
Gul Agha has served as Omar's principal finance officer
and one of his closest advisors. At one time, no one was
allowed to meet with Mullah Omar unless approved by Gul Agha.
He lived in the presidential palace with Omar during Taliban
rule, served as his personal financial secretary, and was one
of Omar's closest advisors.

(3) Name: Amir Abdullah

AKA: Amir Abdullah Sahib

DOB: circa 1972

POB: Paktika Province, Afghanistan

Amir Abdullah has served as the personal treasurer to

senior Taliban leader Mullah Berader (listed on February 23,

2001 under UN 1267) and was the former deputy to the Taliban

governor of Kandahar Province. Amir Abdullah has traveled to

Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, Libya and UAE to fundraise and collect

money for the Taliban. Amir Abdullah also facilitates

communications for Taliban leadership and coordinates

high-level meetings at the guesthouse of his Karachi

residence. Abdullah also helped many senior Taliban members

who fled Afghanistan settle in Pakistan in 2001.

The Haqqani Network is a Taliban-affiliated group of .

militants that operates out of North Waziristan Agency,

Federally Administered Tribal Areas, Pakistan, and has been

spearheading insurgent activity in Afghanistan. The Haqqani

Network leadership consists of the three oldest sons of

Jalaluddin Haqqani, including Nasiruddin Haqqani. The UN

1267 Committee added Jalaluddin Haqqani to its Consolidated

List of terrorists and terrorist supporters on January 31,

2001.

Nasiruddin Haqqani functions as an emissary for the

Haqqani Network and spends much of his time pursuing family

business. From at least 2005 to 2009, Nasiruddin Haqqani

collected funds for the Haqqani

Network through various fund-raising trips, including a 2008

fundraising trip to a Gulf state and during regular travel to

the United Arab Emirates in 2007. As of mid-2007, Haqqani

reportedly had three main sources of funding -- donations
