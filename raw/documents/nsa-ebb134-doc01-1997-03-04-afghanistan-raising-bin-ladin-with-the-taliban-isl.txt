5. (C) EHSAN SUGGESTED BIN LADIN DID NOT HAVE THE MEANS OF

STANAKZAI IMPLIED THAT BIN LADIN, NOT ONLY WAS NOT SUPPLYING VAST
SUMS OF MONEY TO THE TALIBAN AS SOMETIMES ALLEGED, BUT WAS BEING
PED BY THE TALIBAN.
