3. (C) AMBASSADOR SIMONS MET WITH TALIBAN "ACTING
FOREIGN MINISTER" MULLAH MOHAMMED GHAUS ON NOVEMBER 8.
TALIBAN ACTING RECONSTRUCTION MINISTER ATTIQULLAH,

THAT THANKS TO GOD AND HELP FROM ITS
FRIENDS--INCLUDING THE UNITED STATES--THE AFGHAN
RESISTANCE WAS ABLE TO DEFEAT THE SOVIET UNION AND
ITS ALLIES. AFGHANS AS WELL AS THE INTERNATIONAL
COMMUNITY THOUGHT THAT PEACE WOULD RESULT AFTER THE
SOVIET WITHDRAWAL. HOWEVER, "DUE TO A CONSPIRACY OF
THE COMMUNISTS, THE SELFISHNESS OF RESISTANCE
LEADERS, AND FOREIGN INTERFERENCE THIS EXPECTATION
WAS NOT REALIZED, AND THE WAR HAS CONTINUED." GHAUS
EXPLAINED THAT THE DISASTROUS EFFECTS OF THE WAR WERE
FELT EVEN AT THE VILLAGE-LEVEL, WHERE "NO ONE WAS
SECURE BECAUSE EVERYONE WAS ARMED." TO REMEDY THESE
