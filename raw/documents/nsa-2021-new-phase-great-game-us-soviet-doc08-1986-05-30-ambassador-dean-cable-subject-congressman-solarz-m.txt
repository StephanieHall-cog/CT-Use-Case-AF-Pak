NOT GET HERSELF INVOLVED IN THE INTERNAL PAK DOMESTIC
SCENE. RAJIV SAID THAT THE PEOPLE OF PAKISTAN ARE
POSITIVE TOWARDS THE PEOPLE OF INDIA AND THIS FEELING ON

PROBLEMS FOR ZIA.
