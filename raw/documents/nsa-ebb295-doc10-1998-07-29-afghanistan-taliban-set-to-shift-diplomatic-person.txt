SUBJ: AFGHANISTAN: TALIBAN SET TO SHIFT "DIPLOMATIC" PERSONNEL IN
NEW YORK, RIYADH, AND ISLAMABAD
(U) CLASSIFIED BY JOE NOVAK, ACTING POLITICAL COUNSELOR.
REASONS: 1.5 (C)(D).
1. (U) THIS MESSAGE WAS COORDINATED WITH CONSULATE PESHAWAR.
2. (SBU) VARIOUS SOURCES CONFIRM THAT THE TALIBAN ARE SET TO SHIFT
AROUND "DIPLOMATIC" PERSONNEL ASSIGNED TO THEIR OFFICE IN NEW YORK,
AND THEIR EMBASSIES IN ISLAMABAD AND RIYADH. FROM WHAT WE
UNDERSTAND THE SHIFTS INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING: ABDUL HAKIM MUJAHID,
WHO IS NOW "AMBASSADOR" TO PAKISTAN, WILL GO TO NEW YORK; ABDUL
WAHAB, WHO WAS IN NEW YORK, WILL GO TO RIYADH; AND SYED-UR RAHMAN
HAQQANI, IN HIS FIRST TALIBAN "DIPLOMATIC" ASSIGNMENT, TAKES OVER
IN ISLAMABAD. "THE VOICE OF THE SHARI'A," THE TALIBAN'S RADIO
STATION AND OFFICIAL MOUTHPIECE, HAS NOT ANNOUNCED THE CHANGES YET,
BUT ACTING POLCOUNS WAS TOLD BY A TALIBAN SOURCE THAT IT WOULD
SOON.
3. (C) EMBASSY HAS THE FOLLOWING BRIEF BIO-DATA ON THE TALIBAN
"DIPLOMATS" MENTIONED ABOVE: .
-- ABDUL HAKIM MUJAHID: MUJAHID, AFTER A HALF-YEAR IN
ISLAMABAD, IS RETURNING TO HEAD THE TALIBAN'S OFFICE IN NEW
YORK ONCE AGAIN. IT IS NOT CLEAR WHEN HE WILL TAKE UP HIS NEW
