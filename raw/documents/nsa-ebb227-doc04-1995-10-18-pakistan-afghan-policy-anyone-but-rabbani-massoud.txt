2. SUMMARY: DURING AN OCTOBER 17 CALL BY CHARGE AND POLOFF,

DOMESTIC OPPOSITION TO THE KABUL GOVERNMENT. THUS PAKISTAN NOW
FINDS ITSELF IN THE UNCOMFORTABLE POSITION OF BACKING THE ,
TALIBAN. HUMAYUN OPINED THAT IN MANY WAYS A TALIBAN GOVERNMENT
IN KABUL WOULD BE EVEN WORSE THAN THE PRESENT ONE, ADDING THAT .
A STATE UNDER SUCH ULTRA-CONSERVATIVE RELIGIOUS LEADERSHIP . .
