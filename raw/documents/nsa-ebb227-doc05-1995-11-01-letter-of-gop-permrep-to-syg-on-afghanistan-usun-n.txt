SUBJECT: LETTER OF GOP PERMREP TO SYG ON AFGHANISTAN ,
1. FOLLOWING IS TEXT OF OCTOBER 25 LETTER TO UNSYG FROM :
GOP PERMREP AHMAD KAMAL:
BEGIN TEXT:
-- 1. I WOULD LIKE TO DRAW YOUR KIND ATTENTION TO THE
LETTER ADDRESSED TO YOU BY THE VICE-MINISTER FOR FOREIGN
AFFAIRS OF AFGHANISTAN ON 12 OCTOBER 1995, ONCE AGAIN
ALLEGING PAKISTAN'S SUPPORT TO THE TALIBAN AND ITs
INVOLVEMENT IN THE INTERNAL AFFAIRS OF AFGHANISTAN -
(S/1995/866) .
—- 2, IT IS, INDEED, IRONIC THAT THE GOVERNMENT IN
| KABUL, WHICH CONTROLS NO MORE THAN 5 OF THE 32 PROVINCES
OF AFGHANISTAN, HAS REPEATEDLY SOUGHT TO BLAME PAKISTAN
FOR ITS FAILURE TO RESTORE PEACE AND STABILITY IN
AFGHANISTAN. THE AFGHAN ALLEGATIONS REGARDING
PAKISTAN'S SUPPORT TO THE TALIBAN AND INTERFERENCE IN
AFGHANISTAN'S INTERNAL AFFAIRS ARE BASELESS AND
UNWARRANTED. THERE IS STRONG EVIDENCE TO SUGGEST THAT
THE TALIBAN HAD BEEN ABLE TO CAPTURE WESTERN AFGHANISTAN
WITHOUT MUCH RESISTANCE. THIS IS CORROBORATED BY THE
FACT THAT ONLY 25 TO 30 CASUALTIES WERE REPORTED IN THE
FIGHTING FOR THE CONTROL OF HERAT.
-- 3. THE VIRTUAL WALK OVER OF THE TALIBAN OVER LARGE
AREAS OF AFGHANISTAN CLEARLY REFLECTS THE GROWING
DISENCHANTMENT OF THE AFGHAN PEOPLE WITH THE KABUL
REGIME AND ITS DETERMINATION TO RESOLVE POLITICAL
DIFFERENCES THROUGH USE OF FORCE. GENERAL ISMAIL KHAN,
THE FORMER GOVERNOR OF HERAT, IN HIS INTERVIEW WITH THE
BRITISH BROADCASTING CORPORATION HAS CATEGORICALLY
DENIED PAKISTAN'S INVOLVEMENT IN HIS DEFEAT AT HERAT.
RATHER HE HAS BLAMED THE JUNTA IN KABUL FOR THEIR
DICTATORIAL POLICIES WHICH FINALLY LED TO THE DEBACLE AT
HERAT.
—- 4, THE RECENT ADVANCE OF THE TALIBAN IN CHARASTAB ,
HAS ALSO BEEN POSSIBLE OWING TO DEFECTIONS OF THE KABUL
REGIME'S MILITARY COMMANDERS IN THE AREA. THERE HAS NOT
BEEN MUCH FIGHTING AND THE TALIBAN GAINED GROUND
PRIMARILY BECAUSE OF DISAFFECTION WHICH HAS BEEN
GENERATED AMONGST THE PEOPLE OF AFGHANISTAN BY THE
IRRATIONAL POLICIES OF THE JUNTA IN KABUL. .THIS HAS
BEEN WITNESSED AND REPORTED UPON BY THE INTERNATIONAL
MEDIA PRESENT IN KABUL.
-- 5. THE CONTINUED INTRANSIGENCE OF PROFESSOR
RABBANI AND AHMED SHAD MASOUD TO COOPERATED WITH
AMBASSADOR MESTIRI IN THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE UNITED ‘

OVER AFGHANISTAN, THE JUNTA IN KABUL HAS SOUGHT TO

~- 6. WE WOULD STRONGLY URGE THE REGIME IN KABUL TO

AND THE
ORGANIZATION OF THE ISLAMIC CONFERENCE FOR A PEACEFUL
TRANSITION OF POWER IN AFGHANISTAN, BASED ON NATIONAL
| RECONCILIATION.
-- 7. WE WOULD LIKE TO ONCE AGAIN REAFFIRM THE
CONTINUED NEUTRAL, STANCE MAINTAINED BY PAKISTAN IN THE
INTRA-AFGHAN RIVALRIES. WE CONTINUE TO SUPPORT THE
ONGOING EFFORTS OF THE UNITED NATIONS AND THR
ORGANIZATION OF THE ISLAMIC CONFERENCE FOR PEACE AND
CONCILIATION IN AFGHANISTAN. BEING AN IMMEDIATE .
NEIGHBOUR, PAKISTAN HAS A VESTED INTEREST IN-PEACE AND
STABILITY IN AFGHANISTAN.- WE WOULD LIKE TO SEE
AN EARLY
‘RETURN OF NORMALCY TO AFGHANISTAN SO THAT 1.5 MILLION
AFGHAN REFUGEES CURRENTLY ON OUR SOIL CAN RETURN TO
THEIR HOMELAND IN SAFETY AND HONOUR.
—- 8. I SHOULD BE GRATEFUL IF YOU WOULD HAVE THE TEXT .
OF THE PRESENT LETTER CIRCULATED AS A DOCUMENT OF THE
FIFTIETH SESSION OF THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY, UNDER AGENDA :
ITEM 54, AND OF THE SECURITY COUNCIL.
