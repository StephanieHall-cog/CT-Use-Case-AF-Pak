multiple functions-from allowing Bin Ladin to forge 
the organization to acting as the crucible for the 
11 September plot. The Afghan training system: 
• Imparted the skills necessary to carry out terrorist 
operations and fight in jihad.

recruits and operatives by the most senior members 
of the organization.
