1. (C) SUMMARY: A/S INDERFURTH MET PAKISTANI FOREIGN SECRETARY
AHMED TO DISCUSS AFGHANISTAN, JULY 22. AHMED REVIEWED THE PAK/IRAN
PEACE EFFORT, COMMENTING THAT THE INITIATIVE WOULD TAKE TIME TO
MAKE ANY PROGRESS. INDERFURTH EXPRESSED SUPPORT FOR IT, NOTING
THAT PAKISTAN AND IRAN SHOULD ENGAGE IN A SERIOUS EXCHANGE OF VIEWS
ON AFGHANISTAN. AHMED URGED THE U.S. TO SUPPORT THE GOP'S PROPOSAL
TO VACATE THE AFGHAN UN SEAT. INDERFURTH REPLIED THAT THE U.S.
COULD NOT DO SO, ESPECIALLY WHEN THE TALIBAN WERE MOVING IN THE
WRONG DIRECTION ON SO MANY FRONTS, SUCH AS PRESSURE ON NGO'S TO

THE MEETING, AHMED PROVIDED INDERFURTH A COPY OF HIS TALKING POINTS
(SEE PARA 10). END SUMMARY.
2. (C) ON THE MARGINS OF DEPUTY SECRETARY TALBOTT'S VISIT TO
ISLAMABAD, SA ASSISTANT SECRETARY KARL INDERFURTH MET JULY 22 WITH
PAKISTANI FOREIGN SECRETARY SHAMSHAD AHMED. DCM ALAN EASTHAM AND
MFA ADDITIONAL SECRETARY (AFGHANISTAN) IFTIKHAR MURSHED ALSO

SHARIF WAS COMMITTED TO PEACE IN AFGHANISTAN. THIS WAS SYMBOLIZED

BRIEFING ON PAK/IRAN EFFORT
3. (C) AHMED THEN BRIEFED INDERFURTH ON THE PAKISTANI/IRANIAN
JOINT AFGHANISTAN PEACE EFFORT (SEE REF C). THE INITIATIVE HAD ITS
START IN EARLY MAY WITH PM SHARIF'S MEETING WITH IRANIAN PRESIDENT
KHATAMI IN ALMATY DURING THE ECO SUMMIT THERE. AT SHARIF'S
INVITATION, FOREIGN MINISTER KHARAZI HAD VISITED ISLAMABAD IN EARLY
JUNE. THAT VISIT HAD GONE WELL AND BOTH SIDES HAD AGREED TO

AFGHANISTAN. VISITS TO AFGHAN SITES HAD TAKEN PLACE IN JUNE AND
JULY. MURSHED AND (IRANIAN SPECIAL ENVOY ALLAHEDDIN) BORUJERDI
WOULD PROBABLY MAKE A JOINT VISIT TO KANDAHAR AND MAZAR-I-SHARIF

5. (C) A/S INDERFURTH THEN ASKED AHMED WHETHER THE PAKISTANI
GOVERNMENT AND IRAN HAD REALLY HELD FRANK DISCUSSIONS? HAD THE TWO
SIDES REALLY PROBED EACH OTHER'S POSITIONS? FOR EXAMPLE, HAD THE
TWO DISCUSSED THE FACT THAT THEY ARE PROVIDING ASSISTANCE TO
DIFFERENT FACTIONS? IT WOULD HELP IF ECONOMIC ISSUES WERE ALSO
DISCUSSED SINCE SOME OBSERVERS SAID IRAN WANTED THE WAR TO CONTINUE
SO AS TO PREVENT PIPELINE CONSTRUCTION, WHICH IS NOT IN PAKISTAN'S
INTEREST. AHMED REPLIED THAT THE TALKS HAD BEEN FRANK AND HAD BEEN
SO FOR A VERY LONG TIME. HE KNEW THIS WAS THE CASE SINCE HE HAD
BEEN PAKISTAN'S AMBASSADOR IN TEHRAN.

THE TALIBAN ARE ANGERED THAT THEY ARE NOT RECOGNIZED BY THE
INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY AND BLAME THE UN.

SOMETHING ABOUT TALIBAN VIEWS ON THE NGO ISSUER. ON CUE, MURSHED

HAD NOT BEEN ORDERED TO LEAVE; THEY HAD ONLY BEEN ASKED TO MOVE TO

AHMED ALLOWED THAT THE TALIBAN ARE DIFFICULT TO DEAL WITH, BUT THEY
SHOULD BE CAREFULLY DEALT WITH BECAUSE THEY ARE EASILY PROVOKED.
THIS WAS THE CASE PARTICULARLY ON THE ISSUE OF CONVERSION -- MANY
OTHER MUSLIM COUNTRIES WOULD NOT ALLOW SUCH ACTIVITIES. DCM NOTED
THAT IF THE TALIBAN HAD A PROBLEM WITH A SPECIFIC NGO THEY SHOULD
