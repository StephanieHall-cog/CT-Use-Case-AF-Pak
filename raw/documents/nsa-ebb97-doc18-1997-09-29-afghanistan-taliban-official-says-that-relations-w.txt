3. (C) IN AN HOUR-LONG PHONE CONVERSATION WITH POLOFF ON
SEPTEMBER 28, MULLAH ABDUL JALIL, THE TALIBAN’S DEPUTY
FOREIGN AFFAIR’S ADVISER, WHO WAS IN KANDAHAR, SAID THE
TALIBAN’S RELATIONS WITH RUSSIA AND IRAN ARE "TENSE"
AFTER THE SEIZURE OF KABUL BY THE TALIBAN ON SEPTEMBER
27. JALIL BITTERLY ATTACKED THE RUSSIAN GOVERNMENT
STATEMENT ISSUED ON SEPTEMBER 27 CONCERNING THE CAPTURE °
OF KABUL AS "JUST ANOTHER EXAMPLE OF RUSSIAN INTERFERENCE
IN AFGHAN AFFAIRS, WHICH HAS BEEN GOING ON SINCE THEY PUT
THE COMMUNISTS IN POWER IN KABUL IN 1978." (NOTE: THE
RUSSIAN STATEMENT CONDEMNED THE EXECUTION OF FORMER .
PRESIDENT NAJIBULLAH AS A "BARBARIC ACT" AND SAID THAT
THE SEIZURE OF KABUL "ONLY AGGRAVATES THE CRISIS INTO
WHICH AFGHANISTAN HAS BEEN PLUNGED." END NOTE.) JALIL .
PROJECTED THAT THE TALIBAN’S RELATIONSHIP WITH RUSSIA
WILL REMAIN NEGATIVE FOR THE CONCEIVABLE FUTURE. QUERIED
AS TO WHETHER THE TALIBAN HAD HAD ANY CONTACTS WITH THE
TAJIK OPPOSITION, JALIL SAID THEY HAD NOT AND HAD NO
PLANS TO COMMENCE ANY SORT OF RELATIONSHIP IN THE FUTURE.
4. (C) JALIL STATED THAT TALIBAN-IRANIAN RELATIONS ARE
"DEADLOCKED" BECAUSE IRAN "CONTINUES TO RECOGNIZE
COMMANDER MASOOD AND BURHANUDDIN RABBANI AS THE
LEGITIMATE GOVERNMENT OF AFGHANISTAN." ASKED HOW HE KNEW
THIS, JALIL SAID THAT IRAN RADIO’S PASHTU AND DARI ,
SERVICES ANNOUNCED IT ON SEPTEMBER 27. ANOTHER SERIOUS
ISSUE WITH IRAN, JALIL SAID, WAS THAT IT HARBORS " SEVERAL .
THOUSAND ANTI-TALIBAN AFGHANS, WHO IT MAY USE TO INVADE :

COMMENT
11. (C) JALIL SOUNDED ENERGIZED AND BUSY WITH THE TASK
OF CONSTRUCTING A NEW GOVERNMENT FOR AFGHANISTAN. AS
JALIL’S COMMENTS MAKE CLEAR, THE LEGACY OF THE SOVIET .
OCCUPATION OF AFGHANISTAN IS STILL FRESH IN THE MIND OF
THE TALIBAN, AS IS IRAN’S SUPPORT FOR THE MASOOD/RABBANI
GOVERMENT. GIVEN THIS, IT APPEARS THAT IT WILL TAKE SOME
VERY ADEPT DIPLOMACY BY RUSSIA AND IRAN TO NORMALIZE
THRTIR RELATIONS WITH THE TALIBAN IN THE NEAR TERM.
CONCERNING DR. HOLL’S VISIT TO KANDAHAR, WE ARE
ENCOURAGED THAT THE TALIBAN REGARD THE VISIT AS
"POSITIVE," BUT NOTE THAT HE MET WITH ONLY MID-LEVEL .
TALIBAN OFFICIALS NONE OF WHOM IS A MEMBER OF THE INNER
SHURA.
