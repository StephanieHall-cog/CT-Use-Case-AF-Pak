THEMES IN YOUR DISCUSSIONS IN PAKISTAN. THIS CABLE IS LABELED A
SCENESETTER BUT CONSISTS MOSTLY OF JOHN HOLZMAN'S IMPRESSIONS BASED
ON HIS THREE TRIPS TO AFGHANISTAN OVER THE PAST SEVERAL MONTHS.

! ALL'S WELL IN TALIBAN LAND

FORCE IN AFGHANISTAN. IN KANDAHAR, THE CAPITAL OF TALIBAN LAND,
THEY ARE ON THEIR TURF AND RULE EASILY. THE MARKETS ARE FULL BY
AFGHAN STANDARDS (BUT NOT AT ALL IN COMPARISON TO OTHER SOUTH ASIAN

APPARENTLY PLY REGULAR ROUTES. THE ELECTRICITY IS OFF, BUT THE
TALIBAN ARE LOOKING FOR DONOR HELP TO GET A NEARBY HYDEL PROJECT
BACK IN OPERATION. SHROUDED WOMEN ARE FREQUENTLY SEEN SHOPPING IN
THE MARKETS OR RIDING IN HORSE DRAWN TONGAS. BUT MANY OLD WOMEN
AND YOUNG GIRLS DO NOT WEAR BURQAS. AT LEAST DURING ONE AFTERNOON .
IN EARLY RAMAZAN, TRAFFIC ON THE ROADS WAS NOT STOPPED DURING
PRAYERS AND PEOPLE WERE NOT BEING FORCED TO PRAY. OUTSIDE THE

CITY, THE ROADS ARE DEFINITELY SAFER, BUT OTHERWISE TALIBAN
GOVERNANCE IS PROBABLY MINIMAL. MOST VILLAGES CONTINUE TO RELY ON .
TRADITIONAL TRIBAL LEADERS, WHO APPROVE OF THE TALIBAN BUT WOULD
NOT WANT THEM TO DIRECTLY INTERFERE IN THEIR DAILY LIVES. IN

SHORT, LIFE IS NOT SO BAD AND THE PEOPLE OF KANDAHAR WOULD BE CRAZY
NOT TO PREFER TALIBAN THEOCRACY TO FACTIONAL WARFARE AND RULE BY .
BRIGANDS, AS EXISTED IN KANDAHAR FROM 1992-94.
IN KABUL AND HERAT--TALIBAN AN OCCUPYING FORCE

. 4, (C) KABUL AND, WE UNDERSTAND, HERAT ARE A DIFFERENT STORY. IN

THESE CITIES, THE TALIBAN ARE AN OCCUPYING FORCE, GOVERNING PEOPLE
WHO ARE NOT PREDOMINANTLY PASHTOON AND ARE NOT LIKE THE FOLKS BACK
IN KANDAHAR, AND THEIR RULE IS HARSH AND OPPRESSIVE. WE CONTINUE
TO RECEIVE REPORTS THAT THE TALIBAN DETAIN TAJIKS, PANJSHIRIS, AND
OTHERS INDISCRIMINATELY, SHIPPING AT LEAST SOME TO A PRISON IN
KANDAHAR. WOMEN ARE NOT SEEN AS FREQUENTLY ON THE STREETS AS IN
KANDAHAR, APPARENTLY OUT OF FEAR OF BEING ACCOSTED. THE STAFF OF
THE UN HUMAN RIGHTS RAPPORTEUR CLAIMED THAT ONE TALIB LEADER HAD
TOLD THE RAPPORTEUR THAT A WOMAN SHOULD NEVER APPEAR IN PUBLIC

SINCE A MAN OTHER THAN HER HUSBAND MIGHT TAKE PLEASURE IN HER
APPEARANCE AND THUS DISHONOR HER. WE ARE TOLD THAT AFGHAN WOMEN
SENT HOME FROM THEIR JOBS NO LONGER RECEIVE PAY, CONTRARY TO ‘
TALIBAN PROMISES. SCHOOLS ARE NOT OPEN TO AFGHAN GIRLS, BUT OF .

. COURSE THEY ARE NOT OPEN IN KANDAHAR BITHER. IT'S JUST THAT THE .

KABULIS AND HERATIS HAVE MORE OF A TRADITION OF EDUCATING GIRLS.

THE TALIBAN OPERATE MAINLY THROUGH VERBAL--NOT WRITTEN--EDICTS AND
THUS THEIR RULE IS OFTEN CAPRICIOUS AS WELL AS OPPRESSIVE. THE
STORIES OF TALIB ABUSES AGAINST THE CLEAN SHAVEN OR THOSE NOT
WEARING TURBANS ARE LEGION--AND MOST ARE PROBABLY TRUE. THUS IT IS
SAFER TO LIE LOW RATHER THAN RISK THE IRE OF A WANDERING TALIB
MILITIAMAN. IN THE PAST FEW WEEKS MULLAH OMAR HAS TRIED TO CREATE
A KINDER, GENTLER TALIBAN OCCUPATION, WARNING HIS SOLDIERS THAT

THEY WOULD BE BEATEN IF THEY BEAT UP CITIZENS FOR NOT CONFORMING TO

} TALIB MORES.

5. (C}) THE TALIBAN LEADERSHIP ARE DEVELOPING AN ATTITUDE PROBLEM
ABOUT THE WEST AND ESPECIALLY THE UN AND THE NGOS--THE
ORGANIZATIONS THEY DEAL WITH ON A DAILY BASIS, AND THE SOURCE OF
MOST OF THE MEDIA CRITICISM OF THEIR POLICIES. BOTH MULLAH HASAN
AND MULLAH RABBANI--AMONG THE TOP 3 OR 4 TALIB LEADERS-~TOLD BRAD
AND ME THAT THEY CARRIED A GRIEVANCE AGAINST THE NGOS. MULLAH
HASAN, THE ONE-LEGGED GOVERNOR OF KANDAHAR, MADE THE SAME POINT BUT
DID NOT DESCRIBE IT AS A GRIEVANCE. ALL COMPLAINED OF HIGH
HANDEDNESS ON THE PART OF THE UN AGENCIES AND NGOS AND OF’ ~
DISCRIMINATION, E.G. REDUCING AID AFTER THE TALIBAN TAKROVER.
6. (C) WE'VE ALSO HEARD BUCKETFULS FROM THE TALIBAN THAT THEY, AS
THE RULERS OF KABUL, HAVE NOT BEEN RECOGNIZED AS AFGHANISTAN'S
GOVERNMENT. THE UN CREDENTIAL COMMITTEE DEFERMENT, WHICH LEFT
JAMIAT IN THE AFGHAN UN SEAT, WAS AN ESPECIALLY HARD BLOW. THEIR .
SENSE OF BEING "PUT UPON" IS MADE MORE ACUTE BY THEIR CONVICTION
- THAT THEY ACT IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE WILL OF ALLAH, AND THAT THEIR
MOVEMENT WILL DEFEAT THE OTHER FACTIONS AND ESTABLISH ISLAMIC RULE ,
THROUGH OUT THE COUNTRY. THEY CLAIM THAT THEY COOPERATE WITH THE
UN PEACE EFFORT, BUT THAT IS MOSTLY PRETENCE AND AN EFFORT TO
: MANAGE THE WEST. THEY DO NOT WANT TO BE LABELED AT HOME OR ABROAD
AS THE REJECTIONIST PARTY AND THUS PARTICIPATE GRUDGINGLY AND ONLY
TO, ACHIEVE NARROW ENDS. IN THE JUST COMPLETED WORKING GROUP ‘
SESSION THEY REFUSED A RAMAZAN CEASE FIRE BUT AVIDLY PURSUED A
PRISONER EXCHANGE, SINCE TRIBAL LEADERS IN KANDAHAR AND HELMAND ARE
PUTTING PRESSURE ON THEM TO GAIN THE RELEASE OF FIGHTERS CAPTURED :
BY MASOOD. REPORTING IN OTHER CHANNELS INDICATES THAT VARIOUS
RADICAL ISLAMIC GOVERNMENTS AND ORGANIZATIONS ARE APPROACHING THE
TALIBAN, AND WE THINK THEY: ARE PROBABLY MAKING INROADS (E.G. USAMA
, ' BIN LADIN ENJOYING TALIBAN HOSPITALITY). WE CAN _EXPECT THIS TREND
: TO CONTINUE AS THE TALIBAN BECOME MORE DISILLUSIONED WITH THE WEST. ,
