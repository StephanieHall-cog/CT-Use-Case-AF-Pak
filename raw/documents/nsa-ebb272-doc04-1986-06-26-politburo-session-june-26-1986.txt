Politburo Session, 26 June, 1986 
Notes of Anatoly S. Chernyaev 
 
 
About Afghanistan 
 
Gorbachev.  We have reached a new stage in our relations. There is now a new leadership in 
Kabul. Where will it go? The Afghan society has also come through a whole stage, gained some 
experience, the processes will get straighter… And we should act in such a way so that they 
follow our lead. The road is open for them to act independently, and the new leadership there is 
ready and willing to do just that. They have shown themselves to be both wise and skillful and 
able to shoulder these important political decisions. We need to bring our consulate up to speed, 
and change advisors. We need to give the new leadership a chance to step up to the task. We 
need to name a new ambassador and cut in half the number of members of the advisers. There 
are 2500 of them… 
 
And we need to tell Najib: here is our approach. Kornienko (the First Deputy Minister of Foreign 
Affairs) has already told him some of this. 
 
The United States does not want political reconciliation. They’re doing whatever they can to 
make it fail. We have taken a first step and withdrew two regiments… Maybe we should just 
announce: look, two years have passed, we said we would take our forces out and we have 
started doing just that. Maybe we should, in the near future, take out another 5 to 10 thousand to 
give the process a boost. We will complete it within two to three years, in any case. But to do it 
all as a package. We need to think it all through and keep the initiative. [We should] pull U.S.A. 
and Pakistan by their tails all the time. We just need to be sure that the final result does not look 
like a humiliating defeat:  to have lost so many men and now abandoned it all. In other words, 
we need the process to continue, so that it does not pause. 
 
Maybe we should invite Najib to Moscow?  In short, we have to get out of there. 
 
Gromyko: It is not our war. 
 
Gorbachev: It would be harder to blame us for it…. 
 
Gromyko: We need to bring some people into the government from the opposition. We need to 
find individuals. 
 
Gorbachev: We will put the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the KGB, the Defense Department, and 
Dobrynin in charge of preparing such a proposal.
