1. (C) SUMMARY: IN THE PAST SEVERAL MONTHS, MULLAH RABBANI, THE
TALIBAN DEPUTY LEADER, HAS RAISED HIS PUBLIC PROFILE, SEEMINGLY
OVERCOMING REPORTED EFFORTS BY TALIBAN LEADER MULLAH OMAR TO
SIDELINE HIM. IN FACT, SOME OBSERVERS SPECULATE THAT "CLIQUES" OF
RABBANI AND OMAR SUPPORTERS ARE CURRENTLY INVOLVED IN A FACE-OFF
FOR INFLUENCE WITHIN THE MOVEMENT. HOWEVER, MOST OBSERVERS, NOTING
TRADITIONAL TALIBAN SOLIDARITY, DOUBT THAT AN OPEN BREAK WILL OCCUR
IN THE NEAR-TERM, IF AT ALL. AS EMBASSY HAS NOTED BEFORE,
LEADERSHIP RELATIONSHIPS WITHIN THE TALIBAN ARE OPAQUE. THE MOST
THAT CAN BE SAID AT THIS TIME IS THAT RABBANI'S REEMERGENCE COULD .
BE A NET PLUS IF -- AS SOME SPECULATE -- HE IS MORE WILLING THAN
