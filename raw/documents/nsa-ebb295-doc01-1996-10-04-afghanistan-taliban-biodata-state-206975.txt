MULLAH ABBAS
9. (C) MULLAH ABBAS SERVES AS THE TALIBAN'S ATTORNEY GENERAL. IN

MOHAMMADI). ABBAS SERVED AS MAYOR OF KANDAHAR BEFORE TAKING THE
POSITION OF AG IN THE SPRING OF 1996.
MULLAH WAKIL AHMAD
10. (C) MULLAH WAKIL AHMAD WHO IS WIDELY CONSIDERED A MEMBER OF
THE TALIBAN'S INNER OR EXECUTIVE SHURA IN KANDAHAR OFTEN ACTS as
THE MOVEMENT'S SPOKESMAN WITH FOREIGN PRESS REPRESENTATIVES. IN
HIS EARLY THIRTIES, AHMAD IS A MEMBER OF THE KAKAR TRIBE FROM
KANDAHAR CITY. HE WAS A STUDENT AND SUBSEQUENTLY A TEACHER IN A
PAKISTANI MADRASA WHERE HE REMAINED DURING THE JIHAD. HE JOINED
THE TALIBAN IN OUSTING THE KANDAHAR COMMANDERS FROM POWER IN THE
FALL OF 1994.

11. (C) MULLAH YAR MOHAMMAD, WHO HAS BEEN THE GOVERNOR OF HERAT
SINCE THE FALL OF 1995, HAS A REPUTATION FOR BEING MORE OF A
"MILITARY MAN" THAN A DIPLOMAT. THRE GOVERNOR REPORTEDLY BECAME
EMBARRASSED AND CALLED FOR THE ASSISTANCE OF A MULLAH WHEN HERATTI
WOMEN ATTEMPTED TO PETITION HIM AFTER TALIBAN AUTHORITIES CLOSED
GIRLS' SCHOOLS AND PROHIBITED MOST WOMEN FROM WORKING IN HERAT.
IN HIS LATE THIRTIES, YAR MOHAMMAD IS A POPALZAI-DURRANI PASHTUN
FROM THE ARGHISTAN DISTRICT OF KANDAHAR PROVINCE. HE STUDIED IN
MADRASAS IN AFGHANISTAN AND PAKISTAN, LATER BECOMING A COMMANDER
IN THE ARGHISTAN DISTRICT. HE HAS A REPUTATION AMONG AFGHANS AS ,
A BRAVE COMMANDER WHOSE COURAGE WAS REPORTEDLY REWARDED BY A GIFT
OF FIFTEEN STINGER MISSILES. HE WAS ARRESTED BY AHMAD SHAH
MASOOD IN THE SPRING OF 1995 WHEN HE TRAVELLED INTO KABUL
_ GOVERNMENT CONTROLLED TERRITORY AS A PART OF A DELEGATION SENT TO
NEGOTIATE WITH MASOOD. YAR MOHAMMAD WAS RELEASED A FEW MONTHS
LATER IN A PRISONER EXCHANGE ORGANIZED BY PAKISTANI JUI. (JAMIAT-
I-ULEMA-I ISLAM) LEADER FAZLUR REHMAN.
