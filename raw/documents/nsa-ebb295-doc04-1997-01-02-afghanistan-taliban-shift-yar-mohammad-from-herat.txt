3. (C) IN A JANUARY 1 MEETING WITH POLOFF, AFGHAN EMBASSY FIRST
SECRETARY ABDUL WAHAB CONFIRMED THAT TALIBAN LEADER MULLAH OMAR HAS
SHIFTED YAR MOHAMMAD FROM HIS CURRENT POSITION AS GOVERNOR OF HERAT
AND APPOINTED HIM GOVERNOR OF GHAZNI PROVINCE (REF A). ACCORDING
TO WAHAB, COMMANDER ABDUL RAZAQ, A FORMER DEPUTY TO SLAIN TALIBAN
COMMANDER MULLAH BORJAN, HAS BEEN APPOINTED THE NEW GOVERNOR OF
HERAT. HE NOTED THAT MOHAMMAD, A FORMER RESISTANCE COMMANDER WHO
HAD BEEN HERAT GOVERNOR SINCE THE CITY FELL TO THE TALIBAN IN

4. (C) ASKED FOR THE REASON FOR THE SHIFT, WAHAB SHRUGGED HIS
SHOULDERS AND REPLIED "AMIR OMAR DOES NOT LIKE ONE MAN TO STAY IN
ONE POSITION FOR TOO LONG A TIME." HE ADDED THAT MOVING FROM HERAT
TO GHAZNI SHOULD NOT BE CONSIDERED A DEMOTION FOR MOHAMMAD BECAUSE
"GHAZNI IS A LARGE PROVINCE IN A STRATEGIC AREA." POLOFF THEN
ASKED WHETHER MOHAMMAD WOULD BE BRINGING HIS NUMEROUS POPALZAT -

WITH MOHAMMED DURING THE JIHAD AS A MEMBER OF THE HEZB-I-ISLAMI
({KHALIS) FACTION, RESPONDED THAT HE THOUGHT THAT THESE FORCES WOULD
REMAIN DEPLOYED IN HERAT.
