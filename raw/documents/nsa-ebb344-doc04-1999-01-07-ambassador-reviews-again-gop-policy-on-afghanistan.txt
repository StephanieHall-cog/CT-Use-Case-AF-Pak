2. {S}- THE AMBASSADOR MET JANUARY 7 WITH FOREIGN MINISTER SARTAT

AZIZ. DRAWING ON REF A, THE AMBASSADOR UNDERSCORED TWO KEY POINTS.

FIRST, PAKISTAN SHOULD RECONSIDER ITS SUPPORT FOR THE TALIBAN.

THIS POLICY IS GETTING ITS INTERESTS NOWHERE ("HAVE FOUR YEARS OF

ALL-OUT SUPPORT FOR THE TALIBAN IMPROVED PAKISTAN'S POSITION?").

THE SECOND, MOST CRITICAL POINT WAS THE NEED FOR PAKISTAN TO USE

ITS INFLUENCE WITH THE TALIBAN TO CONVINCE THEM TO EXPEL

INTERNATIONAL TERRORIST USAMA BIN LADIN IMMEDIATELY TO A PLACE
WHERE HE CAN BE BROUGHT TO JUSTICE.
