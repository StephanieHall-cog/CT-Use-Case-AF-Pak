1. (U) SUMMARY: THE TALIBAN MADE SIGNIFICANT
ADVANCES NORTH OF THE HINDU KUSH MOUNTAINS DURING THE
PAST YEAR, AND ARE IN POSITION TO MAINTAIN AND

THAT THE TALIBAN ACHIEVED BY THE END THE 2000 FIGHTING

SIDES FACE IN 2001. THE FOLLOWING REGIONS WILL BE
DISCUSSED IN TURN: THE SHOMALI PLAIN, IMMEDIATELY

NORTH OF KABUL; EASTERN AFGHANISTAN; THE NORTHEASTERN
REGION OF BAGHLAN, KUNDUZ, AND TAKHAR PROVINCES; AND
THE NORTH-CENTRAL PORTION OF THE COUNTRY, INCLUDING

THE HAZARAJAT REGION. NOTE: ALL PLACE NAMES REFERRED ,

AFGHANISTAN WHICH SHOWS PROVINCIAL AND DISTRICT
8OUNDARIES. END NOTE. FINALLY, THIS MESSAGE

DISCUSSES THE POSSIBILITY THAT. THE OPPOSITION MIGHT BE
STRENGTHENED IN 2001 THROUGH THE RETURN OF SUCH

FIGURES AS ABDUL RASHID DOSTUM AND ISMAIL KHAN.
