LONGSTANDING TENSIONS BETWEEN TALIBAN LEADER MULLAH OMAR AND DEPUTY
LEADER MULLAH RABBANI HAVE GROWN WORSE. ONE OF THE ISsuES
REPORTEDLY DIVIDING THE TWO IS HOW TO HANDLE TERRORIST USAMA BIN
LADIN: OMAR IS SAID TO BE IN FAVOR OF HIS PRESENCE IN AFGHANISTAN,
WHEREAS RABBANI WOULD LIKE TO HAVE THE MATTER SETTLED. OBSERVERS
DOUBT, HOWEVER, THAT OMAR WILL ALLOW THE PROBLEMS WITH RABBANI --
WHO HAS MANY ALLIES ~- TO RESULT IN AN OPEN BREAK. FOR HIS PART,
RABBANI HAS PUBLICLY DENIED ANY PROBLEMS WITH OMAR. IN THE
MEANTIME, IN WHAT APPEARS TO BE A SIGNAL OF THE SAUDI GOVERNMENT'S

2. (C) TENSIONS BETWEEN TALIBAN LEADER MULLAH OMAR AND DEPUTY
LEADER AND HEAD OF THE KABUL "SHURA" (COUNCIL) MULLAH RABBANI, ARE
REPORTEDLY ON THE INCREASE. ACCORDING TO SOME REPORTS, THE
PROBLEMS BETWEEN THE TWO MEN HAVE GROWN SO DEEP THAT MULLAH RABBANT
HAS DECIDED TO REMAIN IN DUBAI -- WHERE HE HAS BEEN SINCE EARLY
DECEMBER -- FOR THE NEXT SEVERAL WEEKS (SEE PARA FIVE). ‘THE
PRECISE REASONS FOR THE PROBLEMS BETWEEN THE TWO ARE UNCLEAR.
HOWEVER, IN A DECEMBER 9 MEETING WITH POLOFF, BI
TALIBAN SOURCES HAVE PRIVATELY INDICATED TO HIM THAT THE LATEST
PROBLEM DIVIDING THE TWO MEN CENTERS AROUND HOW THE TALIBAN SHOULD
DEAL WITH THE ISSUE OF TERRORIST USAMA BIN LADIN: OMAR CONTINUES
TO TAKE THE HARD-LINE VIEW THAT BIN LADIN SHOULD NOT BE EXPELLED OR
EXTRADITED, WHILE RABBANI BELIEVES THAT THE TALIBAN MUST SETTLE THE
MATTER BEFORE THEY BECOME EVEN MORE ISOLATED FROM THE INTERNATIONAL
COMMUNITY. EXPANDING ON THIS LAST POINT,[—sBTATED THAT
RABBANI IS PARTICULARLY CONCERNED AT THE DETERIORATION OF THE
TALIBAN'S RELATIONSHIP WITH SAUDI ARABIA BECAUSE OF BIN LADIN'S
CONTINUED PRESENCE IN AFGHANISTAN. (NOTE: IN SEPTEMBER, THERE
WERE REPORTS THAT RABBANI, UPSET OVER OMAR'S HANDLING OF THE BIN
LADIN ISSUE, MAY HAVE BEEN INVOLVED IN A COUP ATTEMPT AGAINST OMAR.
THESE REPORTS WERE NEVER SUBSTANTIATED -- SEE REF C.)
