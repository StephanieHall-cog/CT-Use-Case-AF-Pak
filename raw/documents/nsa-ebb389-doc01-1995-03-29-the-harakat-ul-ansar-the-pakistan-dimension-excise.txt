4. THE VIOLENCE IN KARACHI ON FEBRUARY 5, IN WHICH
23 PERSONS WERE KILLED IN A MELEE OF SHOOTINGS AND
BOMBINGS (REP A), FOCUSSED ATTENTION FOR THE FIRST
TIME HERE ON AN ORGANIZATION KNOWN AS HARAKAT UL
ANSAR. AMONG THE SEVERAL VIOLENT INCIDENTS ON THAT
DAY WAS A DRIVE-BY SHOOTING OF A LARGE TENTED .
PAVILION IN CENTRAL LIAQUATABAD, AT WHICH FUNDS WERE
BEING COLLECTED FOR THE SUPPORT OF, IN THE WORDS OF
THE FUNDS COLLECTORS, "JIHAD IN KASHMIR." PRESS
REPORTS OF THIS INCIDENT ON FEBRUARY 6 STATED THAT
THIS PAVILION AND THE FUNDRAISING EFFORT HAD BEEN
ORGANIZED BY HARAKAT UL ANSAR. |

MAJOR POLITICAL PARTIES HERE REVEALED THAT VERY FEW
PEOPLE HERE IN KARACHI HAD EVER HEARD OF HARAKAT UL
ANSAR. IN OUR SUBSEQUENT QUERIES WITH A RANGE OF
_ CONTACTS, WE HAVE PIECED TOGETHER SOME BASIC
