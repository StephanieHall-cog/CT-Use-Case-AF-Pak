SECURITY SITUATION
3. (C) PAKISTAN: THE SITUATION IN PAKISTAN APPEARS BASICALLY
CALM ON AUGUST 24. BASED ON INFORMATION RECEIVED FROM THE
CONSULATES, THERE HAVE BEEN NO REPORTS OF LARGE DEMONSTRATIONS OR
VIOLENCE OF ANY SORT, THUS FAR. CONSULATE KARACHI HAS SPOKEN TO
PAKISTANI SOURCES IN QUETTA, BALUCHISTAN, WHO CONFIRM THAT THE
SITUATION IS QUIET THERE (THERE ARE ABOUT 40 AMCITS IN THE QUETTA
AREA; SOME AMCITS WORKING OUTSIDE OF QUETTA REQUESTED INFORMATION
8/24 FROM RSO ON CHARTER FLIGHTS TO ISLAMABAD; RSO HAS PROVIDED
THEM THIS INFORMATION). (NOTE: BECAUSE OF ITS PROXIMITY TO THE
TALIBAN'S HOME AREA OF KANDAHAR, QUETTA IS OF SPECIAL CONCERN FOR
SECURITY REASONS.) PESHAWAR IS QUIET AS IS ISLAMABAD AND LAHORE.
4. (C) IN LOOKING TOWARD THE FUTURE, THE PERIOD FOLLOWING FRIDAY
PRAYERS PROVIDES ANOTHER OCCASION FOR ANTI-U.S. MULLAHS TO TRY TO '
PUT PEOPLE OUT ON TO THE STREETS. ALSO, IN ISLAMABAD ON
THURSDAY, STUDENTS AND MULLAHS AT THE LOCAL ISLAMIC UNIVERSITY
ARE PLANNING A MARCH TO THE PARLIAMENT. SO FAR, HOWEVER, (WITH
THE EXCEPTION OF PESHAWAR) WE HAVE SEEN NOTHING APPROACHING A
LARGE-SCALE DEMONSTRATION, EVEN IN THE EMOTIONAL IMMEDIATE

7. (C) THE PAKISTANI PRESS CONTINUES TO DEVOTE EXTENSIVE
COVERAGE TO THE U.S. STRIKES, ALTHOUGH THE OVERALL VOLUME IS
CONSIDERABLY REDUCED IN COMPARISON TO OVER THE WEEKEND. ONE NEW
‘STORY THAT IS MAKING THE ROUND OF THE DAILY PAPERS IS A REPORT
THAT AN UNEXPLODED CRUISE MISSILE WAS FOUND IN THE DESERT OF
KHARAN IN BALOCHISTAN. POLCOUNS HAD AN OPPORTUNITY TO EXPLORE
. PUNDIT REACTIONS ON MONDAY WITH LTG (R) NISHAT AHMED, HEAD OF THE
INSTITUTE OF REGIONAL STUDIES, THE LEADING[ id
[ ___—s—~is~=C*d) THINK TANK IN ISLAMABAD. NISHAT IS A GENUINE
MODERATE, CLOSE TO CHIEF OF ARMY STAFF GEN. JEHANGIR KARAMAT, AND
A COMMITTED DEMOCRAT. HE SAID HIS CONTACTS AMONG THE PAKISTANI
FOREIGN POLICY COMMUNITY UNDERSTOOD THAT THE UNITED STATES FACED
A SIGNIFICANT PROBLEM FROM TERRORISM AND NEEDED TO TAKE ADEQUATE
STEPS TO PROTECT ITSELF. THE SENSE, HOWEVER, WAS THAT THE U.S.
HAD MOVED TOO QUICKLY TO RETALIATE AGAINST BIN LADIN WITHOUT
